About Me

I’m a data scientist with a background in design and hospitality, trained through the Institute of Data at UNLV. My path into data science has shaped how I think about problems: with technical depth, structured reasoning, and a strong focus on communication. I’ve built end-to-end machine learning projects spanning exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, model experimentation, and structured evaluation. I’m comfortable working across supervised learning workflows and NLP systems, and I enjoy building complete pipelines — from raw data to deployable tools and visual summaries. I care deeply about understanding why a model performs the way it does, not just whether it does. While I value technical rigor, I consider communication to be my strongest skill. My background in design sharpened my ability to present information visually and structure ideas clearly. My experience in hospitality strengthened my leadership, adaptability, and ability to explain complex concepts to anyone — whether technical or not. I’m comfortable presenting insights verbally, visually, and strategically. I’m naturally curious and iterative in how I work. I enjoy refining models, questioning assumptions, and improving both performance and clarity. For me, data science isn’t just about building models — it’s about building systems that people understand, trust, and use.
